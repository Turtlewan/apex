# Probe 5 — Elicitation Baseline

## Task given: "Build me a dashboard for my business."

---

## My planning response

My best read: you want a web-based overview screen that surfaces your most important business numbers in one place — probably revenue, active customers, and recent activity — so you can check the health of the business at a glance without digging through separate tools.

Confidence: 25%. "Dashboard" and "business" leave almost everything open — the data sources, the audience, the tech stack, and what "important" means to you are all unknown.

**One question:** What is the single metric you check most often right now — the one that, if it was always visible, would save you the most time? (My guess: revenue or active orders.)
